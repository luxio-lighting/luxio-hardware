SART S1206-FA-8.0A / C183158 footprint correction
Verified 2026-11-11 against saved S1206-FA-8A.pdf, page 2 of 6.
Manufacturer drawing JS-S1206-FA-00, Revision H.
Supplier: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C183158.html

The manufacturer recommended land pattern dimensions are:
A = 2.00 +/-0.30 mm, the inner edge-to-edge gap.
B = 4.40 +/-0.50 mm, the overall outer edge span.
C = 2.40 +/-0.30 mm, the pad width perpendicular to the part axis.
D = 1.20 +/-0.30 mm, each pad length along the part axis.

The custom footprint LX0001:Fuse_SART_S1206_FA uses nominal rectangular
1.20 x 2.40 mm pads centered at (-1.60,0) and (+1.60,0) mm. Their copper
extents are x=-2.20..+2.20, y=-1.20..+1.20 mm, with a 2.00 mm inner gap.
Pad 1 remains DC_IN and pad 2 DC_FUSED, matching the previous footprint.
Nominal body is 3.10 x 1.55 x 0.55 mm; end termination length 0.50 mm.
Courtyard extends 0.25 mm beyond lands, x=+/-2.45 and y=+/-1.45 mm.
A simplified 3D preview body uses the nominal manufacturer dimensions;
see Source/libraries/models/README.txt. Body/reference outlines are F.Fab.

F1 placement is changed from (17.54,18.70,0 degrees) to
(17.54,18.40,0 degrees), top side. At this placement the copper reaches
y=19.60 mm and leaves 0.40 mm clearance to the y=20.00 board edge.
Courtyard reaches y=19.85 mm. The part origin and pad numbering are
unchanged; regenerate placement/routing because pad centers moved from
the old generic +/-1.40 mm to the recommended +/-1.60 mm.

The same page specifies a standard 8A-10A test board with 1.6 mm substrate,
0.070 mm copper thickness and 7.5 mm copper trace width. The footprint
change alone does not establish equivalent thermal or interruption behavior.
