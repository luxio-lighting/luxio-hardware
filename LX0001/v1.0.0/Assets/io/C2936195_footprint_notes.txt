HDGC TYPE C-CQ-105PWB / C2936195 USB-C footprint verification
Reviewed 2026-11-11

Selected to replace HRO C165948 because the latter has through-hole metal
shell stakes that conflict with the input terminal on the opposite side.
The selected HDGC part has four entirely surface-mounted shell tabs.
It still has two plastic locating pegs. These must NOT be removed or ignored.

Source: manufacturer drawing A0, 2016-03-12, part TYPE C-CQ-105PWB, A001.
Supplier: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C2936195.html
Assembly stock: https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C2936195
PDF: https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/d49f8d5338b4308d8b987c3cfefc00ca.pdf?productCode=C2936195

Electrical: 3 A, 20 V connector rating; USB 2.0 16-contact receptacle.
JLCPCB snapshot: 655 stocked and available; USD 0.1977 each at 100 pieces.
Connector rating does not authorize drawing 3 A from an arbitrary USB source.

Footprint: LX0001:USB_C_HDGC_TYPE_C_CQ_105PWB
Native top view has mouth toward +Y. Origin is the nominal body center.
Body length 7.35 mm, width nominal 8.94 mm; outer shell-tab width 11.44 mm.
The 8.94 mm body width is inferred from the drawing scale/8.34 mm opening
and two 0.30 mm shell walls; it is used only for F.Fab/courtyard display.
The solder pattern is taken from the explicitly dimensioned PCB drawing:
- Four shell lands 2.00 x 2.00 mm, x = +/-4.77, y = -3.125 and +0.775.
  The 7.54 mm dimension is between inner shell-pad edges, giving 9.54 mm
  center spacing and 11.54 mm total copper width. Row spacing is 3.90 mm.
- Locators x = +/-2.89, y = -2.605, holes diameter 0.60 mm. The drawing's
  locator center is 0.52 mm forward of the signal-pad bottom/rear-shell
  centerline, consistent with the 6.28 mm mouth-to-locator dimension.
- Signal-pad bottom coincides with rear-shell centerline, and the signal
  pad top with the rear-shell top edge in the recommended drawing. Thus
  the land length is 1.00 mm (half of the 2.00 mm shell-pad length).
  Centers y = -3.625. Shared power/GND lands width 0.60 mm, centers
  x = +/-3.20 and +/-2.40. The remaining eight lands are 0.30 mm wide,
  centered at x = -1.75,-1.25,-0.75,-0.25,+0.25,+0.75,+1.25,+1.75.
  Signal-pad length is established by the drawing's aligned edges rather
  than a standalone numerical length dimension. Confirm final JLC model
  alignment during the assembly preview.
- Paired A1/B12, A4/B9, A9/B4 and A12/B1 names use coincident pads to match
  KiCad's standard USB2.0 16P symbol; each pair must be on the same net.
- A8 and B8 are unused SBU contacts and should be marked not connected.
- Copper extents x = +/-5.77, y = -4.125 to +1.775 mm.
- Courtyard x = +/-6.02, y = -4.375 to +3.925 mm.
- Nominal mouth y = +3.675 mm. An approximate local 3D preview model is
  attached; inferred shell width/height and simplified cosmetic geometry
  are documented in Source/libraries/models/README.txt.

Recommended placement for the project's 60 x 20 x 1.6 mm PCB:
- Input terminal J2 top: center (6.50, 15.30), orientation 270 degrees.
- USB J1 bottom: center (3.175, 12.30), flip before setting 90 degrees.
  This is the sequence used by Source/tools/generate.py.
- This places the USB mouth at x = -0.500 mm, exactly 0.5 mm beyond x=0.
- USB locator centers become (5.780,9.410) and (5.780,15.190).
- The HDGC terminal front copper lands span y=12.30..14.30 and
  y=16.30..18.30. Both USB holes lie outside these copper lands.
- The closer hole at y=15.190 has 0.590 mm clearance to the nearest
  terminal copper edge, including its 0.300 mm drill radius.
- Plastic pegs extend 0.75 mm below the USB mounting surface. A 1.6 mm
  PCB leaves nominal 0.85 mm before the opposite PCB surface, so pegs
  do not reach the top-mounted terminal. This mechanical overlap is
  intentional; the holes may lie within the opposite-side courtyard.
- All metal shell attachment is bottom-side SMT; no USB metal stake
  extends through the PCB into the terminal footprint.
- USB copper minimum y is 12.30-5.77=6.53 mm. This clears a 6 x 6 mm
  antenna corner. Recheck any enlarged RF keepout against the actual
  copper extents, including PCB/placement tolerances.
