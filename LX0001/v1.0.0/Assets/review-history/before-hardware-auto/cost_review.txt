5 A / 48 V connector rating. Peg-free; four 0.80 +/-0.20 mm shell stakes

advertisement and the controller's configured USB limit still apply.

ACTIVE PART AND DIODE CHECKS
C2135 JSCJ BAT54C, SOT-23, common cathode: pins 1 and 2 are separate
anodes; pin 3 is common cathode. Verified visually in manufacturer page 1
and against KiCad Diode:BAT54C. Connection to BUTTON_COMMON on 3 pulls
both BOOT and GPIO18 low through independent diodes when SW1 closes.
Do not replace with BAT54A(common anode) or BAT54S(series).

COST DECISION
Retain the selected verified parts. No lower-priced substitute was declared
pin-compatible without manufacturer evidence. Savings from replacing a part
must include JLC extended-part setup fees and not just its unit price.
