LX0001 USB copper design basis — 0.30 mm drill revision

Updated UTC: 2026-11-11T16:05:32.301121+00:00
USB load current bound used: 1.384 A, the reviewed upper TPS2553 high-current clamp.
Wired 5 A spines are separate and must not be narrowed to these USB values.

Primary guidance: Texas Instruments SNVA054C (AN-1229), section 6.1, pages 10–11.
https://www.ti.com/lit/an/snva054c/snva054c.pdf
Local PDF SHA256: 2ddd88ce573c9a68a0b171ac18de40c3e32870b0711cc2502795c1837e99e35c
TI relates current capacity to permitted temperature rise and ambient/component heating.
Its 1 oz outer-copper rule of thumb for moderate rise yields 0.42184 mm at 1.384 A.
Prefer 0.45 mm or wider for sustained USB routing;0.6 mm is retained where room permits.
Short 0.4 mm package escapes can be assessed by their actual resistance and copper loss.
Neither a width rule nor the resistor-network model establishes enclosure temperature.

Computed copper values at 20 C,35 um thickness, rho 1.724e-8 ohm-m:
Width mm   Resistance mOhm/mm   Drop mV/mm at 1.384 A   Loss mW/mm
0.35       1.407347              1.947768                2.695711
0.40       1.231429              1.704297                2.358747
0.45       1.094603              1.514931                2.096664
0.60       0.820952              1.136198                1.572498

For a 0.4 mm trace, 50 mm total series length is 61.57 mOhm and 0.1179 W at 1.384 A.
That is a length illustration, not a measured or final LX0001 route.
At higher copper temperature the resistance/loss increase; manufacturing tolerances remain.
A 0.30 mm finished drill with 20 um plating and 1.6 mm barrel has about 1.37 mOhm copper resistance.
At1.384 A this is only a few mW, but final model must include actual via count and current sharing.

Final-board evidence workflow:
Run Source/tools/review_current_paths.py --board <final-board> --include-usb after zero-gap DRC.
This extracts saved pads/tracks/filled zones and models USB_5V J1→U4, USB_LIMITED U4→Q3,
VSYS Q3→Q6, LED_VCC Q6→J4 and GND J3→J1 at 1.384 A.
Report actual PCB hash, dimensions, path resistance, voltage drop, losses and limitations.
USB cable, connector contacts, TPS2553 switch resistance and MOSFET channels are separate losses.
Bench validation in the actual case remains necessary for final temperature qualification.
