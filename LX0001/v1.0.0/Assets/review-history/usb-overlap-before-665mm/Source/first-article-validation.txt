LX0001 v1.0.0 — first-article acceptance

These measurements have NOT been performed. CAD/ERC/DRC checks do not establish
the 5 A continuous thermal rating, antenna match, USB compliance or assembly yield.
Build a small first-article lot before releasing all 100 boards.

1. Assembly and recovery
Compare JLCPCB's placement preview against assembly drawings, especially the
custom terminal, USB, fuse, MOSFET and antenna footprints. Inspect QFN wetting,
paste bridges, crystal orientation and the recessed USB shell stakes. Check shorts before
power-up. Use a current-limited supply and confirm approximately 3.3 V and 5.0 V
at the controller and AHCT rails. Hold SW1 and pulse ESP_EN low; confirm ROM USB
download and UART recovery. Read back the 4 MB flash identifier; disable PSRAM.

2. Power paths
Test USB alone, wired alone at 5/12/24 V, and both sources in both insertion orders.
Measure input/output voltages and reverse leakage into each disconnected source.
With 5.0 V wired and 5.2 V USB, wired must retain priority. Check handover for
oscillation, overshoot and resets; a reset-free handover is not guaranteed.
USB alone must leave LED_VCC off until firmware explicitly enables a 5 V strip.
With a wired source present, LED_VCC must follow that source automatically.
Verify the USB current-limit bands listed in firmware-interface.txt, source
advertisement sensing, enumeration/suspend behavior and LED brightness limiting.
Use a USB analyzer or suitable test source; do not infer compliance from USB boot.

3. Load and temperature
Measure loaded input/output voltage and component/connector/solder temperatures
at 1 A, 3 A and 5 A using 5, 12 and 24 V wired supplies. Allow thermal equilibrium
in the intended enclosure and at the intended worst-case ambient. Compare the
measured temperature rise with actual component, connector and enclosure limits.
Record fuse, Q1, Q6 and regulator temperatures and thermal-camera images. The 8 A
fuse is fault protection; it is not a precise 5 A output-current limiter. Verify
fault clearing with the intended external supply and wiring. Copper-resistance
estimates exclude connector contacts, solder joints and hot-device resistance.

4. Data, clock and RF
Scope both buffered outputs at the terminal using representative strip cables.
Check 5 V amplitude, edge ringing, idle-low behavior and simultaneous channels.
Verify no data drive before the buffers' DATA_ENABLE sequence. Exercise SW1 as
GPIO18 input and confirm it does not clamp other signals or reset the running MCU.
Start flash at 40 MHz DIO; perform repeated reads/writes and cold boots over the
input-voltage and temperature range before selecting 80 MHz/QIO operation.
Measure crystal frequency/startup. Tune RF and antenna matching with a VNA and
validate Wi-Fi performance in the final enclosure. The installed matching values
are initial values, not measured values for this PCB and enclosure.

5. Release record
Record hardware/source revision, BOM substitutions, firmware revision, input
supplies, cables, case, ambient, instruments and results. Update the RF BOM if
tuning requires it, rerun ERC/DRC/netlist checks and regenerate all manufacturing
outputs after any layout or component change. Archive the accepted JLCPCB quote,
placement preview and first-article results with the production release.
