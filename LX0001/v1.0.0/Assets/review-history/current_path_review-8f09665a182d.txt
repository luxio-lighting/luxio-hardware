Copper DC model
Rasterized actual filled zone polygons, tracks and copper pads on both layers
on a0.1mm square grid. Modeled each cell edge as one copper square and solved
the two-layer conductance network with fixed1V/0V terminal-pad potentials.
The finite-volume equation was solved by diagonally preconditioned conjugate
gradients; residual norm below1e-7 in all cases. No synthetic unfilled pours
were counted. Assumed20°C copper resistivity1.724e-8 ohm-metre. Reported both
35µm and70µm copper because the snapshot has no explicit stackup definition.
Via barrel model:1.6mm board,20µm copper wall, actual0.30/0.20mm drill sizes.
A0.30mm drill gives approximately1.372mΩ/barrel;0.20mm gives1.996mΩ.
Those are modeling assumptions, not verification of the fabrication order.
Same-number connector pads and same-device source/drain pads are treated as
equipotential terminals. Internal metal/contact and solder resistance are
excluded, as are the fuse element and MOSFET on-resistance. Copper resistance
increases with temperature. Grid discretization can miss very small necks.
