BOM headers Comment, Designator, Footprint, LCSC Part #, Quantity are suitable.
Comma-separated designator groups and per-board quantities match parts.json.
DNP parts and bare test pads/mounting holes are excluded from assembly.
CPL headers Designator, Mid X, Mid Y, Layer, Rotation contain millimetre
coordinates, Top/Bottom side names and native KiCad rotation values. JLC's
KiCad guide does not require an additional bottom-coordinate mirror.
Custom terminal and USB footprint origins are centered on their bodies.
Supplier-specific zero-angle alignment still requires review of the JLC
placement preview and assembly drawing; native KiCad angles alone do not
prove the orientation used in every supplier catalogue.

The accepted placement exports 109 distinct active assembly references:
52 top and 57 bottom. These exactly match 109 active BOM references, with no
missing or extra designators. Hybrid SMT/PIP USB J1 is included by --smd-only
because its footprint is classified as an SMT assembly part. J2/J3/J4 are
also included. Gerber, Excellon and CPL share the lower-left origin at CAD
(-6.5,20). The retained CAD outline is x=-6.5..60 and y=0..20. Exported X is
CAD X+6.5; exported Y is 20-CAD Y. For example:
J1 CAD(-3.75,10) -> CPL(2.75,10), Bottom, 90 degrees;
J2 CAD(6.5,10) -> CPL(13,10), Top, -90 degrees;
C4 CAD(21.4,10.3) -> CPL(27.9,9.7), Bottom, 0 degrees.
Gerber copper order: F.Cu / In1.Cu / In2.Cu / B.Cu.
Order the exact four-layer stackup in fabrication-intent.txt.

Fabrication export is gated by zero unrouted connections, zero schematic
parity issues, clean ERC and the independent connectivity/paste audit.
Routing-via drills require at least 0.10 mm clearance from every actual paste
aperture, with a 1 nm numerical allowance. No routing-via paste exception exists.
No DRC findings or exclusions are accepted. All four USB mounting holes now
clear J2. The geometry checker recognizes the four shell stakes intersecting
their OWN component-side PIP paste apertures; this is normal component
mounting, not a courtyard exception or a routing-via-in-paste exemption.
Gerbers are generated in a fresh staging directory before the archive is
written, preventing stale files from entering a new fabrication package.
This review does not bypass those gates or claim prototype qualification.

Silkscreen uses direct top-side GND/V+ input labels and GND/D16/D17/V+ output
labels, outside terminal housings and paste lands. BOOT is above the centered
button; IN/OUT identify the connector ends. IN is at (3.6, 4.9), clear of the
relocated R1/R12 and the recessed USB stakes. Bottom branding is two lines:
  LUXIO LX0001
  v1.0.0 5-24V
It is centered at (22.95, 1.55); actual strokes occupy x 18.7607..27.1012 and
y 0.3933..2.6313, clear of the relocated bottom R103. All 10 labels use 0.8 mm
text height and 0.15 mm strokes. Top and mirrored-bottom render inspection
passed, with zero silk/text DRC findings. Rerun add_silkscreen.py after any
component or exposed-via move and validate the final promoted board.

0.30 mm drill revision (version remains v1.0.0):
All 246 routing vias now have 0.30 mm holes. The 206 smaller via pads
remain 0.45 mm; the 40 larger pads remain 0.60 mm. JLCPCB publishes a
minimum via pad-minus-drill difference of 0.10 mm, with 0.15 mm preferred:
https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities/
Minimum annulus is explicitly 0.075 mm; all other copper, hole spacing,
antenna and independent 0.10 mm drill/paste constraints are retained.
C114 shifted +0.05 mm in X; R102 shifted -0.02 mm in X; R100 shifted
-0.02 mm in X / -0.04 mm in Y. CPL and placement.json were regenerated.
Local BOOT and DATA_ENABLE traces were adjusted. RF signal traces, crystal
traces and the main 5 A power spines were preserved. Detailed item changes
are recorded in via030-revision.json; the previous release is archived in
Assets/review-history/via020-before-030-revision.

66.5 mm USB mounting revision, still v1.0.0:
J1 moved -6.5 mm in X and the left board edge moved from 0 to -6.5 mm.
Both input connectors retain y=10 mm. USB shell masks are open on both faces,
and top fillets have no opposite-side component obstruction. USB and CC nets
were reconnected; obsolete stubs removed. The USB_5V short front bridge uses
0.40 mm copper and its final copper loss is re-evaluated. U2 is the USB ESD
protector; its ground island now has a direct tented via to reference ground.
Main 5 A, RF and flash tracks are retained. All 249 vias have 0.30 mm drills.
The exporter requires ZERO DRC findings; the former four-item allowlist is gone.
See usb-mounting-revision.json for current geometry and archived release.
