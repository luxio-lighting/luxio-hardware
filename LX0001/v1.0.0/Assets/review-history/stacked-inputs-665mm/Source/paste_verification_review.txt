Independent drill / solder-paste verification

Source/tools/verify_design.py checks physical drill shapes against the actual
front and back paste apertures. Net equality never suppresses an overlap.
Routing-via drills must have at least0.10 mm paste clearance by default,
with only a1 nm numerical allowance at the exact boundary. The via-in-paste
allowlist is empty. This catches holes whose centers are
outside a pad but whose drill edges intersect its paste, and paste-only
exposed-pad stencil windows. The PCB is never saved or modified by the check.
Evidence is Export/manufacturing-geometry-check.json and .txt, with PCB and
checker SHA256 values. Re-run on the final routed PCB.

Current project paste margins are zero; native rotated pad shapes therefore
match the apertures exactly. Expanded/reduced ordinary pads use KiCad 10's
published paste-sizing rules on detached copies. Unsupported expanded
custom shapes, paste text or paste zones cause failure instead of omission.
The script also checks component drills against other components' paste.
Nine validation cases are recorded in
Assets/power/manufacturing-geometry-validation.json. They include the actual
three original same-net GND vias inside J2's paste, clean repaired geometry,
a partial drill-edge overlap, a clear edge, positive/negative paste margins,
and a via inserted into an isolated ESP32 exposed-pad paste aperture.
Separate boundary cases accept nominal0.10 mm and reject0.09 mm.
--minimum-via-paste-clearance-mm 0 runs overlap-only diagnostic cases;
production/export uses the default0.10 mm gate.

USB shell distinction
J1 uses USB4215-03-A and the manufacturer's four plated slotted shell lands.
Their own bottom-side paste apertures are the documented pin-in-paste
assembly intent, not routing vias placed beneath SMT terminals. Only those
four own-pad intersections are recognized, with exact connector/footprint
identity, slot dimensions/positions, GND net, 1.6 mm board thickness, bottom
paste, both mask openings and absent top paste checked. This does not exempt any via
or a component hole crossing another component's paste aperture.
The 66.5 mm revision places all shell holes outside the opposing J2 courtyard.
Top-side annuli are open and fillets are unobstructed. No overlap exception
or slot-tenting assumption remains. Normal first-article solder inspection
still applies; this geometry check does not prove reflow yield.
