LX0001 v1.0.0 power design review — 2026-11-11

Scope and status
This is a design and document review, not a measured production qualification.
The design target is 5 A continuous LED output from a regulated 5–24 V wired
supply. USB supplies the controller and, when firmware enables it within the
source budget, a 5 V strip. No circuitry identifies a strip's rated voltage.
12/24 V strips require the corresponding wired supply. The 5 V boost powers
only the logic buffers; it does not increase the strip supply voltage.

Implemented files
power_parts.json contains 29 power-path parts, all group=power. Existing
regulators, logic buffers, USB sensing and ESD parts are in io_parts.json.
CMSA80P06A_DFN5x6.kicad_mod is the selected strip MOSFET footprint. All its
drain leads and central exposed drain are numbered 5, matching the symbol.
The Si7141DP symbol is used for its pin map only; the actual BOM part is
CMSA80P06A, LCSC C22364965. MMBT5551L is the KiCad symbol name; the actual
Q7/Q8 orderable part is JSCJ MMBT5551(RANGE:200-300), C2145.

Wired and USB source selection
DC_IN -> F1 -> DC_FUSED -> Q1/U3 ideal diode -> VSYS.
USB_5V -> U4 current limiter -> USB_LIMITED -> Q3/U5 ideal diode -> VSYS.
U3/U5 LM74700 pins: 1 VCAP, 2 GND, 3 EN, 4 CATHODE, 5 GATE, 6 ANODE.
Q1/Q3 source pads1–3 connect to the respective input, gate4 to GATE, drain5
to VSYS. C5/C7 are 100 nF between VCAP and ANODE, not from VCAP to ground.
The two 60 V MOSFETs are oriented so their body diodes permit input-to-VSYS
startup and block VSYS-to-input current when their controllers turn them off.

D1/R12/Q2 detect wired voltage and pull U4 EN low. D1 cathode=pin1 faces
DC_SENSE_R, anode=pin2 faces DC_FUSED. This provides wired priority even
when wired input is 5.0 V and USB is 5.2 V. With DC absent, R10 enables USB.
The priority sense is before Q1, so VSYS cannot hold wired-present falsely
active through the reverse-blocking path. Q3/U5 separate the 6 V USB switch
from the 24 V system rail. Retain this stage: a Schottky substitution would
reduce already limited low-USB startup headroom and needs a new leakage and
reverse-voltage analysis. There is no validated low-cost drop-in alternative
identified here. Switching transients and reverse current require bench tests.

USB current selection and firmware requirements
U4 TPS2553DBVR: 1 IN, 2 GND, 3 EN, 4 active-low FAULT, 5 ILIM, 6 OUT.
FAULT is an unused open-drain indication in this design.
R11+R31=60.2 kohm; Q5 off selects the default limit. USB_HIGH_CURRENT turns
Q5 on and puts R32=30.1 kohm in parallel, giving 20.0667 kohm. R33 makes
the high-current setting default off through reset. Q5 pins=1G,2S,3D.
Calculated TPS2553 limits including resistor ±1%:
  default: minimum 388.6 mA, typical 437.2 mA, maximum 492.8 mA;
  high: minimum 1.186 A, typical 1.279 A, maximum 1.384 A.
These are current-limit trip bounds, not guaranteed usable load budgets.
Allow margin below the minimum limit and account for controller consumption.
High mode requires a confirmed Type-C source advertising at least 1.5 A.
The 3 A advertisement does not raise this hardware limit further. CC ADC
thresholds must include Rd tolerance and calibrated ESP32 ADC error.
USB default current, enumeration and suspend rules still require firmware;
the approximately 493 mA hardware ceiling does not enforce the 100 mA
preconfiguration state or suspend consumption. Keep LEDs and Wi-Fi off
until permitted by the available power budget. A 0.6 A peak on 3.3 V can
exceed the default USB budget at low VBUS after conversion losses.

Strip enable and 12 V gate clamp
Q6 source=VSYS, drain=LED_VCC. Its body diode points LED_VCC-to-VSYS, so
it blocks forward supply when off. This is not a reverse-blocking output;
do not externally power LED_VCC. R34 pulls the gate to source (default off).
D5 BZT52C12 cathode1=VSYS, anode2=STRIP_GATE clamps |VGS| near12 V.
Q7 or Q8 sinks the gate through R35=2.2 kohm/0.25 W. Both NPNs use
1 base,2 emitter,3 collector; the 160 V rating gives ample drain-node margin.
Q7 is controlled by USB_STRIP_EN; Q8 automatically enables on wired power.
R38 is 10 kohm, changed from47 kohm during audit to improve saturation
margin at wired5 V. R36 is10 kohm; both base nets have100 kohm pulldowns.
At24 V, R35 dissipates about65 mW and the zener about65 mW. R38 dissipates
about50 mW. At5 V there is no zener conduction and |VGS| is about4.7 V.
At the lowest USB voltage, |VGS| can be below the MOSFET's4.5 V RDS(on)
test point; USB current is limited, but this corner needs measurement.
R40 discharges the unloaded output slowly; it is not an active discharge.
Firmware must keep DATA_ENABLE low until LED_VCC is valid, set data pins
low before enabling the buffers, and disable data before removing strip
power. This prevents data-line parasitic powering of an unpowered strip.

Regulators and power dissipation
U6 LMR51410YFDBVR uses pins1 BST,2 GND,3 FB,4 EN,5 VIN,6 SW.
The100 kohm upper and32 kohm lower divider targets3.3 V from its0.8 V FB.
U7 MT3608 uses1 SW,2 GND,3 FB,4 EN,5 IN,6 NC. The73.2 kohm/10 kohm
divider targets4.992 V from its0.6 V FB. D2 cathode faces+5V_DATA.
LMR51410 requires at least4.0 V input for guaranteed operation. USB cable,
TPS2553 and ideal-diode drops must leave that at VSYS during controller
startup and Wi-Fi bursts. Low-VBUS testing is essential.

CMSA80P06A is60 V with±20 V gate rating. Maximum room-temperature RDS(on)
is15 milliohm at−10 V and23 milliohm at−4.5 V. At5 A that is0.375 W or
0.575 W respectively, before hot-resistance increase. Q1's9.9 milliohm
maximum at10 V adds roughly0.25 W at5 A. F1 contributes about0.17 W from
its6.7 milliohm typical cold resistance. Provide substantial copper around
all three; these numbers do not prove an enclosed20×60 mm PCB's rating.
F1 S1206-FA-8.0A is an8 A/32 V fast fuse with150 A interrupt rating. It
is a fault fuse, not a precise5 A limiter. Its documented8 A reference
board uses70 micrometre copper and7.5 mm traces. The input supply must
also support controller current in addition to5 A LED current, especially
at5 V. Use the prescribed trace/pour design and test steady temperature.

Q6 footprint basis
The manufacturer's CA07N2 page4 provides package geometry, not a suggested
land pattern. Lands were derived from5.85×5.05 mm nominal body,6.15 mm lead
span and1.27 mm lead pitch. Central drain land=3.6×4.2 mm, six paste windows
giving about62% coverage. Confirm solder joints and stencil performance
on first articles. Do not substitute an arbitrary SO8 footprint.

Validation performed and remaining
Exported the generated hierarchical schematic with KiCad10 to XML netlist.
All342 explicitly assigned pins matched parts.json exactly (129 parts at
review time). Compared library pin names against manufacturer pin tables
for LM74700,CSD18543Q3A,TPS2553,LMR51410,MT3608,MMBT5551 and2N7002;
no mismatches found. R38's value-only change does not alter connectivity.
This does not replace routing/DRC or hardware testing. Measure5 A thermal
rise at5/12/24 V in the actual enclosure, voltage drops, source hot-plug
priority at5.0 V DC versus5.2 V USB, reverse leakage, USB low-VBUS startup,
current limits, output inrush and short-circuit/fuse behavior. Validate
data-high voltage and signal integrity with real strip cables.
