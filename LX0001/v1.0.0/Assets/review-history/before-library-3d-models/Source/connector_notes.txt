Dimensioned manufacturer data (mm):
- 2 circuits; 4.00 pitch (A=4.00 +/-0.20, pin pitch +/-0.10).
- Body width B=7.90 +/-0.25, body length 11.50 +/-0.25.
- Maximum nominal body height 4.50 +/-0.25 above board.
- Rear metal contact projects 1.60 beyond body.
- Recommended PCB lands: two 2.00 x 3.50 rear lands, two 2.00 x 6.00
  front lands, with a 4.50 gap between facing front/rear land edges.
- Therefore the corresponding front/rear land center spacing is 9.25,
  and the overall copper length is 14.00.
- Manufacturer rating: 9 A AC/DC, 320 V AC/DC, -25 C to +85 C,
  contact resistance 20 milliohm maximum. The connector rating alone
  does not establish the assembled PCB's continuous current rating.

Coordinates use KiCad's top view, zero rotation, with +Y down the page:
- Origin is nominal plastic body center.
- Wire entry points toward +Y; rear projecting solder tails face -Y.
- Pin 1 is the left circuit (x=-2.00), pin 2 the right (x=+2.00).
  These are assigned circuit numbers: the unpolarized manufacturer's

- Rear pads: x=+/-2.00, y=-6.10, size 2.00 x 3.50.
- Front pads: x=+/-2.00, y=+3.15, size 2.00 x 6.00.

- Nominal body: x=-3.95 to +3.95, y=-5.75 to +5.75.
- Courtyard: x=-4.25 to +4.25, y=-8.10 to +6.40.
- F.Fab WIRE text labels the entry end. Pin-1 chamfer is a CAD convention.
- Rear and front pads of each pin must receive the same net. Prefer copper
  connections to both lands; do not rely on the terminal to bridge a PCB rail.
- A simplified local 3D preview model is attached. Its housing envelope
  follows the drawing; cosmetic details are approximate. See
  libraries/models/README.txt. It is not a manufacturer enclosure model.

Drawing registration limitation:
Pad sizes, X pitch, and Y gap are explicitly dimensioned. The manufacturer
does not numerically dimension the body-to-land longitudinal offset. The
body/F.Fab and origin position relative to the dimensioned lands follow
the drawing scale (rear pad center 0.35 mm behind the rear body edge).
This affects fabrication outline/placement origin registration, not the
dimensioned copper land geometry. Confirm the final assembly orientation
and placement center against JLCPCB's component model before release.

Placement implications:
- Two output bodies side by side occupy 15.80 mm nominal width without gap.
- Our courtyards require at least 8.50 mm between adjacent footprint origins;
  this yields 16.40 mm total body width and fits a 20 mm board.
- Keep wires and insertion/removal tool access clear beyond the entry end.
- For this 60 x 20 mm board with connectors at its short ends, rotate these
  footprints so wire entry faces the board end. Check final pad order from
  the component-side view rather than assuming the rotation's pin order.
- This footprint was independently derived from the HDGC drawing, not copied
  from the WAGO 2060 footprint present elsewhere in the repository.
