LX0001 v1.0.0 — fabrication and assembly intent

Board: 60.0 x 20.0 mm, four copper layers, FR-4, 1.6 mm nominal.
JLC04161H-7628: 35 um outer copper, 15.2 um inner copper.
L1 signals/power; L2 predominantly ground with local signal/power routing
outside the protected RF reference area; L3 signals/ground; L4 signals/power.
Dielectrics: 0.2104 mm 7628 / 1.065 mm core / 0.2104 mm 7628. Black solder mask BOTH sides; white silkscreen. Lead-free
ENIG finish for fine-pitch QFN/RF assembly. Components on BOTH sides. Supply
a panel with tooling rails/fiducials suitable for JLCPCB standard two-side PCBA;
array/rail design belongs to the assembler and must preserve the RF keepout.
Do not change copper thickness without rechecking QFN clearances and RF geometry.

M2 mounting: two 2.2 mm NPTH at (9.5,8.0) and (42.0,3.0) mm measured from the
top-left board corner. Use heads/standoffs no larger than 4.4 mm diameter.
The associated copper/trace/via clearance is 4.6 mm diameter. Add enclosure
strain relief for wiring and USB insertion. No metal/case wall over the antenna.
The BOOT/USER button actuator is centered at (30.0,10.0) mm.
CAD outline: x=0..60, y=0..20. Gerber, drill and CPL share origin (0,20);
CPL is (CAD X,20-CAD Y). Both input connectors are on TOP at the left end.

Input J2 and output J3/J4 are HDGC 2060-452-HDGC, JLCPCB C55251566. Connector
pin numbering is assigned by this design because the mechanical part is
unpolarized. Use schematic, top assembly drawing and silkscreen for wiring.
Viewed from the top with input left/output right, output order from top to
bottom is GND, GPIO16 (5 V logic), GPIO17 (5 V logic), VCC. Input is GND then
VCC from top to bottom. Output VCC follows selected input voltage; it is not
regulated to a strip's required voltage. Choose a matching external supply.

J1 USB-C is on top next to J2; DEALON USB-TYPE-C-018, C2927038, rated3A/5V.
Its four0.65mm-deep metal shell stakes use plated slots in the1.6mm PCB.
Two0.50mm plastic locating pegs use0.60mm NPTH holes. All six holes clear
other pads, paste and routed copper. Shell mask openings are on BOTH sides;
F.Paste is on the TOP component side only. Normal hybrid SMT/PIP assembly
applies; no mounting DRC exception is required.
J1 center(2.50,5.25), rotation-90 degrees, mouth x=-1.175mm.
J2 center(6.50,15.25), rotation-90, wire entry face x=0.75mm.
The connector front-face difference is1.925mm; allow for this in the case.
See Assets/io/C2927038_footprint_notes.txt and manufacturer Rev A drawing.
Use the body-centered CPL origin and verify the assembler's library origin
against the mechanical drawings for both USB and HDGC connectors.

Minimum electrical clearance 0.127 mm. Ordinary signal width is 0.15 mm;
reviewed short, low-current escapes may use 0.127 mm (including the short BUCK_FB_LOW neck).
USB power includes short0.30/0.40mm branches and internal-layer segments; see
the full-stack copper estimate in Source/current_path_review.txt. Main wired
input and ground spines are 2.0 mm, with a 1.8 mm approach to the fuse.
NPTH-to-copper minimum 0.20 mm. USB shell holes are plated slots.
Minimum drill is 0.30 mm. Signal vias use 0.45/0.30 mm (pad/drill);
power and ground banks retain 0.60/0.30 mm where specified in the board.
The 0.075 mm radial annulus on 0.45 mm vias meets JLCPCB's preferred
0.15 mm pad-minus-drill diameter difference. Order the 0.30 mm minimum
via-hole option; no 0.20 or 0.25 mm drill remains.
Tent ordinary vias with mask. Keep routing-via drills
at least 0.10 mm from solder-paste apertures. Do not expose through vias in QFN solder
paste apertures without a filled/capped-via process and updated stencil review.

The ceramic antenna is Walsin RFECA3216060A1T, C127620, at (14.5,1.5) mm.
Preserve its Type1 8 x 3 mm middle-edge clearance window: x=10.5..18.5 mm,
y=0..3 mm. L2/L3/bottom copper, pads and vias are prohibited there; top copper
contains only the deliberate manufacturer antenna lands and feed pattern.
The split FEED/GND lands and opposite tuning land are specified by Walsin
ASC_RFECA3216060A1T_V09 page 3, saved as
Assets/core/Walsin-RFECA3216060A1T-V09.pdf. L104 is an optional DNP antenna
tuning position; do not populate its 0-ohm candidate without RF measurement.
Maintain continuous L2 ground beneath the RF feed outside this window.
L2 x14..20, y3..8.5 mm is protected against signal routing.
The grounded coplanar feed is 0.306 mm wide with 0.200 mm ground clearance;
JLC calculator nominal 50 ohms on L1 referenced to L2 for this stackup.
The unreferenced antenna-window exit and matching pads are transitions.
The router-only reference-ground protection must not remove the final ground
pour. See Source/antenna_routing_proposal.json and Source/core_notes.txt.
Matching values are initial reference values. Antenna matching, feed impedance
and radiated performance need first-article measurement in the intended case.
The short feed exit differs from the manufacturer's test-board geometry.
A stackup calculation does not replace RF matching measurements or radio
certification. The former RFANT corner antenna is superseded.

JLCPCB BOM/CPL use selected supplier C-codes. Cost/stock evidence is a dated
snapshot, not a reservation. The component-only estimate excludes PCB, ENIG,
assembly, setup, feeder fees, attrition, tax and shipping. Obtain the live quote
and review assembly rotations, substitutions and bottom-side mirroring before
accepting a manufacturing order. No fabrication order is placed by this task.
