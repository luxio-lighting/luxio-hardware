LX0001 v1.0.0 — automatic hardware power review

Design review only; assembled electrical and thermal qualification remains open.
Wired design target: 5 A strip load, 5–24 V regulated input. USB design ceiling:
3 A total at 5 V, bounded by connector/cable/adapter ratings, not an onboard clamp.

Paths
DC_IN -> F1 -> DC_FUSED -> Q1/U3 ideal diode -> VSYS -> J4 VCC.
USB_5V -> U4 TPS22965 load switch -> USB_SWITCHED -> Q3/U5 ideal diode -> VSYS.
The separate Q6 strip switch and Q7/Q8 gate drivers are removed. VSYS is directly
routed to output, so USB automatically supplies 5 V to a connected strip.
There is no strip-voltage identification; 12/24 V strips need matching wired power.

D1/R12/Q2 detect DC_FUSED and pull USB_ENABLE low; R10 enables USB when wired
power is absent. This gives wired priority even for wired 5.0 V versus USB 5.2 V.
U4's ON input is hardware-controlled. U3/U5 EN remains connected to its own anode.
LM74700 shutdown alone cannot disconnect forward current through the MOSFET body
diode, hence U4 is retained as a switch. It has no current-limit control.
Q3/U5 isolate U4 from the 24 V VSYS rail and block backfeeding into USB.

U4 TPS22965DSGR (C122837) pin audit against TI SLVSBJ0F:
1/2 VIN = USB_5V; 3 ON = USB_ENABLE; 4 VBIAS = USB_5V; 5 GND;
6 CT = USB_SLEW; 7/8 VOUT = USB_SWITCHED; exposed pad9 = GND.
C18 = 1 nF / 50 V, from CT to ground. At 5 V, approximate 10–90% rise time
is (0.38*1000 + 34) us/V * 5 V = 2.07 ms. This controls startup slew, not
steady load current. C6 is placed beside U4 and provides input bypass. VIN/VBIAS range covers 5.25 V USB.
TPS22965 output discharge is isolated from VSYS by Q3's reverse-blocking path.
No Type-C controller or ESP32 current sensing is fitted; R1/R2 retain passive Rd.
The external adapter must provide overload/short-circuit protection for USB power.

U8/U9 OE pins are grounded. R18/R29 pull GPIO16/17 low during high-impedance reset.
The 5 V buffer rail derives from VSYS through the 3.3 V buck and logic boost.
There is no separately switchable strip rail or GPIO data-enable transistor.
Disconnect board power before connecting strip cables; power sequencing and
startup/shutdown waveforms still need measurement with representative strips.

Regulators and remaining protection
U6 LMR51410: 1 BST,2 GND,3 FB,4 EN,5 VIN,6 SW. Divider 100k/32k targets3.3 V.
Minimum guaranteed VIN is4.0 V: measure VSYS headroom under low VBUS and cable drop.
U7 MT3608: 1 SW,2 GND,3 FB,4 EN,5 IN,6 NC. Divider73.2k/10k targets4.992 V.
Only the data buffers use the 5 V boost; LED power is not converted.
U3/U5 LM74700: 1 VCAP,2 GND,3 EN,4 CATHODE,5 GATE,6 ANODE.
Q1/Q3 CSD18543Q3A: source1–3,gate4,drain5. C5/C7 connect VCAP to ANODE.
F1 8 A/32 V fuse protects the wired path; it is not a precise5 A limiter.
At5 A Q1 loss is up to0.248 W at25 C using9.9 mOhm; F1 typical cold loss0.168 W.
Q6 loss is eliminated. At3 A TPS22965 typical16 mOhm loss is0.144 W, plusQ3,
copper and connector losses. Use current_path_review.txt for extracted copper.
These estimates do not establish temperature limits in the intended enclosure.
