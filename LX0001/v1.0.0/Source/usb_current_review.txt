LX0001 — USB power without onboard current limiting

User requirement: assume a suitably rated ordinary 5 V adapter; no firmware
current negotiation or current-limit selection. USB automatically powers strip.
TPS22965 is a load switch for wired priority, not a current limiter. Passive
5.1k CC resistors enable USB-C source attachment; there is no PD negotiation.

The connector design ceiling is3 A total including controller input current.
The adapter and cable must support the actual load and provide fault protection.
5 A LED operation belongs to the wired input. Do not infer a USB5 A rating from
the load-switch IC's6 A headline rating or removal of the former1.384 A clamp.

Fresh full-stack copper model uses3 A for the USB path and5 A for the wired path.
USB_5V J1->U4; USB_SWITCHED U4->Q3; VSYS Q3->J4; GND J3->J1.
Layer thicknesses35/15.2/15.2/35um and actual vias/filled copper are included.
Results are keyed to the saved PCB hash in Export/current-path-model.
Copper model excludes connectors, switch and MOSFET channels, cable and solder.
Measure temperature, voltage drop, low-VBUS startup, short circuit and source
handover in the actual case before qualifying the intended load.
