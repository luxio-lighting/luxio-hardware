LX0002 v1.0.0 ordering settings

Board: 42 × 20mm, 1.60mm FR-4, 2 copper layers.
Copper: 1oz outside on both layers.
Mask: black; silkscreen: white.
Surface finish: OSP. Standard +/-0.2mm outline tolerance.
Minimum via drill: 0.30mm. Both-side tenting requested for routing vias.
The MCU exposed-pad mask opening exposes its thermal-via tops; the holes
clear the paste windows. Tenting flags do not override component mask openings.
Assembly: TOP SIDE ONLY, 55 fitted parts; no bottom-side assembly.
Upload Gerber ZIP, JLCPCB-BOM.csv and JLCPCB-CPL.csv from the same package.
The per-board footprint is 42 × 20mm; assembly rails may enlarge the production
panel quoted by JLCPCB. Let JLC add the necessary rails/fiducials. Request
depaneling before delivery when finished individual boards are needed.

No impedance-control order option is specified. The short RF feed was modeled
and reviewed for this nominal 1.6mm two-layer stackup; the antenna, matching and
actual RF performance still require first-article testing in the final case.
Do not change stackup thickness or copper weight without reviewing the layout.

Retain electrical testing of the bare boards. Confirm production files and
component placement for the first run, comparing polarity/pin 1 and connector
orientation with the included assembly drawing. This does not approve
unreviewed factory substitutions or a modified footprint.

J1 is USB-TYPE-C-018/C2927038: top-side SMT contacts with plated shell stakes.
Its normal pin-in-paste apertures are intentional; no routing-via-to-paste
exceptions are present. L104 is not populated. Testpads and H2 are not BOM parts.

Electrical use: USB 5V only, direct strip supply, no power-path diode or current
limiter. J1 is rated 3A total including controller, subject to adapter/cable
capability and actual assembled temperature. No USB PD or current negotiation.
Both CC pins have independent 5.1kΩ pull-downs. Use only 5V strips.

Start with first articles and the included validation checklist. Zero CAD
findings do not demonstrate power, RF, firmware or enclosure qualification.
