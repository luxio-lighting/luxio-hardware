# LX0002 first-article validation

Use this checklist on assembled boards before committing to a larger batch. CAD, schematic-parity and copper models validate the saved design; they do not replace measurements on manufactured hardware. Record PCB revision, serial/sample number, firmware build, adapter/cable, ambient temperature, strip type/length, instrument settings and results. Prefer at least three assembled samples.

LX0002 accepts nominal 5V through USB-C only. Do not apply the LX0001 24V supply. The 3A connector limit includes both controller and strip current. Set load steps using measured total USB input current, not strip current alone. Use a suitable regulated 5V supply/cable for load tests; a computer USB port is for programming and low-load checks.

| Check | Setup | Acceptance / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assembly | Inspect J1, U1, U6, U8/U9, R106, crystal, antenna, D4 and connectors against assembly drawings. Inspect QFN exposed-pad soldering where possible. | Correct parts/orientation; no bridges, lifted parts, unsoldered stakes or damaged connector contacts. |
| First power | No strip attached; nominal 5.0V bench supply through USB-C, initially limited to 1A. Measure USB_5V, 3.3V and VDD_SPI at C108/C109. | No persistent current-limit operation or unexplained heating. Target 3.3V DC within ±5%; MCU supply stays inside 3.0–3.6V. In-package flash supply stays inside its 2.7–3.6V operating range. |
| USB and flash | Use both USB-C plug orientations. Confirm 4MB physical flash and 2MB initialized PSRAM, then flash, reboot and run firmware at least 10 times each with the existing 4MB DIO80MHz target. Exercise memory-heavy animations and verify that PSRAM remains available. Exercise the application's USB data commands while Wi-Fi is active. | All programming/data operations complete; no enumeration failures, unexplained disconnects or timeouts. Record firmware/host versions. |
| BOOT / button | Perform 20 cold starts with SW1 released. Hold SW1 during power-up for 10 bootloader entries. Press/release during normal operation and observe GPIO18. | Normal boot when released; download mode only when held during reset/power-up; GPIO18 reports the button without an unintended running-system reset. |
| Rail transients | Probe 3.3V near U1/C103, the left bypass bank and C105 after R106, plus USB_5V at a buffer. Use a short probe ground. Capture startup, Wi-Fi transmission and LED load changes. | All MCU supply domains, including the analog rail after R106, remain 3.0–3.6V; buffer supply remains 4.5–5.5V. No brownout/reboot or sustained ringing outside these limits. A multimeter alone cannot establish transient margins. |
| LED outputs | Test D16 and D17 independently, then together, with supported 5V strips and the intended maximum data-lead length. Exercise off, each channel, full white, rapid changes and animations. | Correct colors and frame timing; no visible flicker, random pixels or reboot over at least 30 minutes. Scope near the first LED if there are errors; compare pulse timing with that strip's datasheet. |
| Power copper | With a suitable load, increase total USB input current through 0.25A, 0.5A, 1A, 2A and the intended maximum up to 3A. Kelvin-measure J1 VBUS-to-J4 VCC and J3 GND-to-J1 GND drops directly on board copper. | No unstable contact, reset or unexpected heating. Compare resistance/drop with `current_path_review.txt`, scaled to actual current and copper temperature. Investigate a board-only loop drop above 150mV at 3A or a large sample-to-sample difference. Cable/connector contact loss must be measured separately. |
| Closed enclosure | Install the final PCB and PLA case. Run worst intended load, Wi-Fi and both outputs for at least 60 minutes, until temperatures change less than 1°C over 10 minutes. Repeat at the highest intended ambient. | No deformation, creep, clip release or thermal shutdown. At 25°C ambient, use 45°C as a conservative external-case target. Plastic in contact with the PCB must stay below the selected filament's continuous-service limit with margin. Check component temperatures against their datasheets. Record USB connector, input neck, buck, MCU and case temperatures. |
| RF in case | Use the same AP, channel, distance and board orientation for open-board and closed-case trials. Log RSSI and throughput while LEDs run; test intended installation positions. | Stable association and application control without unexplained resets/disconnects. Investigate a repeatable enclosure penalty greater than 6dB or unstable throughput. Tune the antenna network if needed; do not infer RF performance from DRC. |
| Mechanical | Fit/remove the PCB, operate the flexible button, insert the USB plug in both orientations and access both wire-clamp release openings. Cycle the case clips and connectors at least 20 times. | PCB retained without bending or metal-to-copper contact; button returns freely; USB fully seats; clamp tools reach their intended openings; no cracked PLA or weakened retention. |

Voltage limits follow the [Espressif power guidance](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32s3/schematic-checklist.html), [Espressif in-package flash specifications](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32_s3_datasheet_en.pdf) and [TI SN74AHCT1G125 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74ahct1g125.pdf). These are operating limits, not permission to overshoot absolute maximum ratings. The drop, case-temperature and enclosure-RF thresholds are engineering screening targets for this project; they are not component certification limits. Any failed item needs a documented cause and a repeat test after correction.

The 3.3V local necks are narrower than Espressif's preferred main-power routing. The final exact ground audit measures 95.2756919258% contiguous bottom-ground coverage under the exposed-pad projection, with a full front ground pad and nine central ground vias. Static BOOT routing occupies the outer-right 0.28mm rim plus two fill-rounding corner bounds of at most 0.05mm; no missing ground lies outside those bounds. This is a documented deviation from Espressif's complete under-chip ground recommendation. The dedicated FH4R2 exposed-pad/via pattern, local bypassing and measured CAD ground coverage reduce risk but do not prove transient/thermal performance. Preserve the measurement record with the production revision and repeat affected checks after PCB, firmware, antenna, connector, cable or enclosure changes.
